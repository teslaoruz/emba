## Sounds

The sound effects in `assets/sounds/` come from [Kenney](https://kenney.nl)'s asset packs
and are in the public domain ([CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)).
`dev/sounds_from_kenney.sh` shows which file each one comes from.
